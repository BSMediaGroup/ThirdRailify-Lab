Generate

Connections → add your Replicate token → Save → Test → choose a model → load controls → enter a prompt → Generate. Paid provider work starts only when you submit.

Compose a thumbnail

Select a result or import an image, then choose Compose. Add a headline, drag it into position, adjust the image framing and shade, then export PNG or JPEG. Fit entire image is the default; Fill is an explicit crop.

Research desk

Drag the divider to resize, double-click it to reset, or use its arrow keys (Home/End for limits). Collapse the left inspector to its icon rail. Pop the research desk into a window or tab. GPT and Grok text chat are optional and use separate local conversations. No web-search tool is connected. Google Images opens externally.

Keep your work

Original images, thumbnail exports, jobs and saved sessions live in .data/. Back up that folder and your private .env. The archive contains no keys or font files. Brand assets are read from poc/assets, the Lab repository assets, then sibling Public/Admin assets. American Captain.ttf or .otf is the title font; labs0.svg is the header motif. Nothing is copied into the update.

Not the deployed application

There is no Admin login, Turnstile, multi-account isolation, public webhook receiver or Cloudflare deployment. Do not tunnel this POC. Read docs/SETUP.md for full key/webhook steps and the production boundary.
